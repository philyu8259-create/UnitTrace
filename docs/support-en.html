Common Questions

  • Does UnitTrace upload my photos? No. The MVP stores photos and reports locally on your device.
  • Can I use UnitTrace offline? Yes. Core inspection, signing, PDF export, and manifest generation are local-first.
  • Is UnitTrace legal advice? No. UnitTrace helps organize evidence records but does not provide legal advice.
  • Why does the app ask for location? Location is optional and is used only when you want evidence records to include approximate location metadata.

Data and Reports

Generated PDFs and JSON manifests are saved locally. You control where they go when using the system share sheet.
